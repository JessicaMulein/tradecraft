# Requirements Document

## Introduction

Tradecraft (working title) is a single-player, text-based espionage game that runs fully locally against models served by LM Studio. The player is a case officer posted to one city under a cover identity. They must uncover and disrupt a hostile Plot. They do this by walking the city, watching people, talking to NPCs, recruiting and running Assets, servicing dead drops, breaking intercepted messages, reading the papers and HQ cables, grading sources, and spotting double agents. All the while a Hostile Service hunts their network and their own station may hold a mole.

**Setting.** The game is set in the early Cold War. The slice's city is Vienna in the 1950s, with the texture of the four-power occupation: sectors, the international Inner City, and Allied and Soviet presence. Places and period detail are real or real-inspired. All characters are fictional, and no real historical individual appears as an NPC or is given dialogue. Further real cities and the full period content model are specified in `.kiro/specs/content-expansion/`.

The governing design rule is that **a deterministic Simulation owns all ground truth and language models only voice characters, describe what the Sim supplies, classify intent and transcribe what was said.** No model can create, change or reveal facts it was not given. This makes the mystery real and reproducible, and it makes it resistant to prompt manipulation.

Every piece of text the player sees is one of two kinds:

- **Fact**: generated by the Sim from deterministic templates (Fact Lines, documents, Case File entries). Facts are what the player can rely on as observations.
- **Flavour**: texture written by a model (NPC speech and Narrator prose). Flavour is guarded against naming unknown entities and inventing specifics, and it is never parsed into facts. NPC speech becomes Claims only through the Claim Extractor, and Claims are assertions, not facts.

This spec covers the first playable vertical slice: one city, one Station, one Hostile Service, one Cell, one Plot, 10–14 Principal NPCs plus Background NPCs, Side Threads and Rumours. All content comes from versioned Content Packs so later specs can add content without code changes.

Out of scope for this slice, each planned as a follow-on spec:

- **Content packs expansion**: more archetypes, location types, personas, documents and cover identities.
- **Plot library**: a broad set of Plot templates beyond the core pack's starter set.
- **Ambient world**: a living city with daily routines, events and news beyond what the slice generates.
- **Campaign and career layer**: chained postings that consume the Outcome Record.
- **Multiple cities and services.**

Also out of scope: multiplayer, voice I/O, graphics and cloud models.

## Glossary

- **Sim**: The deterministic simulation engine. It is the only component allowed to read or write ground truth.
- **Truth Store**: The engine-private store of ground-truth facts, true allegiances, and claim truth values.
- **Case File**: The player's record of Claims, Intercepts, grades, links and notes. It never contains ground truth.
- **Player View**: All engine state the UI is allowed to read, including the Journal, Map view and People view. It is a strict subset that excludes the Truth Store.
- **Proposition**: A canonical fact of the form (subject, predicate, object, optional place, optional time window). It uses the Predicate Vocabulary and entity ids from the Entity Registry.
- **Predicate Vocabulary**: The set of predicates defined by the loaded Content Packs.
- **Claim**: A Proposition asserted to the player by a source (an NPC, an Intercept, surveillance or a document), as recorded in the Case File.
- **Observation**: A Sim-generated record of something the player directly perceived (a sighting, a meeting, an item, a transmission). Observations are always true at their observed time.
- **Fact Line**: A deterministic, template-rendered sentence describing an Observation or an action result.
- **Flavour**: Model-written descriptive text. It carries no facts and is never parsed.
- **Narrator**: The component that writes Flavour for locations, scenes and action results from Sim-supplied Fact Lines and scene descriptors.
- **Specifics Guard**: The check that rejects Flavour sentences containing day names, clock times, dates, numerals or proper names absent from the supplied Fact Lines and scene descriptor.
- **Knowledge Slice**: What one NPC (or the Station) knows or believes: true Propositions, false beliefs, and known entities.
- **Told List**: The Propositions an NPC has already asserted to the player.
- **Cover Story**: The Propositions a concealed NPC presents as true about themselves.
- **Cover Identity**: The player's own legend (for example a trade attaché), which affects first contacts and Cover Suspicion.
- **Cover Suspicion**: The Hostile Service's suspicion that the player is an intelligence officer.
- **Agenda**: What an NPC is trying to conceal or promote in conversation.
- **Leak Guard**: The component that checks model output, sentence by sentence, for registered entities outside an allowed set.
- **Intent**: A classified category for a player's dialogue line, from a fixed set.
- **Principal NPC**: An NPC with a role in the Plot, the Hostile Service, the Station or the player's starting contacts.
- **Background NPC**: A civilian NPC with no role in the Plot. Background NPCs carry local facts, Rumours and Side Thread knowledge.
- **Unidentified Subject**: A player-side placeholder for an observed person whose identity the player does not know, shown by physical descriptor.
- **Contact Channel**: A means for the player to reach an NPC remotely (to arrange meetings or task them).
- **Cold Approach**: Opening a conversation with a present NPC who has no Contact Channel with the player.
- **Walk-in**: An NPC who contacts the Station unprompted. A Walk-in may be genuine or a Dangle.
- **Asset**: An NPC recruited by the player.
- **Double Agent**: An NPC whose apparent allegiance differs from their true allegiance.
- **Dangle**: A Hostile Service agent who volunteers to the player in order to deceive them.
- **Chickenfeed**: Low-value true information, or deception, passed through a Double Agent to build credibility.
- **Hostile Service**: The opposing intelligence service. It is a deterministic AI inside the Sim, not a language model.
- **Cell**: The group of NPCs executing the Plot.
- **Plot**: The hostile operation, made up of ordered Plot Stages with deadlines and prerequisites, instantiated from a Plot Template.
- **Side Thread**: A self-contained, true, Plot-unrelated storyline (for example smuggling or an affair) that produces traces and red herrings.
- **Rumour**: A false or distorted Proposition circulating among Background NPCs and in newspapers.
- **Noise Traffic**: Communications traffic unrelated to the Plot.
- **Location**: A place in the city with a Location Type, District, opening hours, crowd levels and a risk rating.
- **District**: A group of Locations. Travel between Districts costs phases.
- **Route**: A connection between Districts with a travel cost in phases.
- **Dead Drop**: A concealed site at a Location used to pass items without meeting.
- **Channel**: A communications path (radio, numbers broadcast, courier, or dead drop) with a schedule and an owner.
- **Exposure**: How visible an Asset's relationship with the player is to the Hostile Service.
- **MICE**: The recruitment motivation model: Money, Ideology, Coercion, Ego.
- **Admiralty Grade**: A two-part grade: source reliability (A–F) and information credibility (1–6).
- **Station**: The player's own service in the city: the Chief of Station and staff.
- **Chief of Station**: The player's handler. The Chief briefs, issues Directives, answers cables and controls funds.
- **Directive**: An objective issued by the Chief with a deadline and a Standing reward.
- **Standing**: The player's reputation with HQ.
- **Budget**: The player's operational funds, kept as a ledger.
- **Cable**: An in-world message between the player and HQ.
- **Dossier**: An HQ file on an entity, holding apparent information only.
- **Document**: Any in-world readable text: newspaper, public text, Dossier, Cable or seized material.
- **Starting Brief**: The generated opening package: Cover Identity, known entities, initial leads, Dossiers, Channels, Dead Drops, Directives and Budget.
- **Journal**: The player's chronological log of Fact Lines, plus their own notes.
- **Content Pack**: A versioned directory of data files (archetypes, location types, templates, predicates and so on) validated at load.
- **Content Set**: The merged content of all loaded Content Packs.
- **Content Manifest**: The list of loaded packs with id, version and content hash.
- **Difficulty Preset**: A named set of concrete tuning values (easy, standard, hard).
- **Outcome Record**: The versioned end-of-game record kept separately from saves, for a future campaign layer.
- **Hostile Memory**: The part of the Outcome Record describing what the Hostile Service learned about the player's network.
- **Custody**: The period after a Station arrest during which the Station holds the arrested NPC.
- **Turning**: Recruiting a hostile agent so that it secretly works for the Station while appearing loyal to the Hostile Service.
- **Feed**: Passing chosen Propositions to the Hostile Service through a turned agent.
- **Abort Pressure**: The Plot's accumulated disruption count, compared against the Hostile Service's abort tolerance.
- **Implicating Claim**: A Case File Claim that, under its predicate's implication rule, ties a target to a hostile organisation, hostile person, hostile Channel or Plot materiel.
- **Notification**: A player-visible message, with a Fact Line, produced by an event addressed to the player or the Station.
- **Turn Transaction**: The single commit of every state change produced by one player action or dialogue line.
- **Model Roles**: `voice` (high-stakes dialogue), `fast` (routine dialogue and intent classification), `narrator` (Flavour), `bookkeeping` (claim extraction), and `judge` (evaluation scoring).
- **LLM Gateway**: The single component that talks to the OpenAI-compatible model endpoint.
- **Reference Machine**: Apple M4 Max MacBook with 64 GB unified memory, running LM Studio.

## Requirements

### Requirement 1: Seeded World Generation

**User Story:** As a player, I want each campaign generated from a seed, so that every playthrough has a fresh but reproducible web of agents, cells and plots.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a new game starts with a seed THEN the Sim SHALL generate a city, the Station, one Hostile Service, one Cell, one Plot, 10–14 Principal NPCs, and the Background NPCs, Side Threads and Rumours set by the Difficulty Preset, using only the seeded PRNG and the loaded Content Set.
2. WHEN the same seed, generator version, Content Manifest and Difficulty Preset are used THEN the Sim SHALL produce an identical World State.
3. THE Sim SHALL assign every NPC a true allegiance, an apparent allegiance, a MICE profile, a persona, a physical descriptor, a schedule, a Cover Story where applicable, and a Knowledge Slice.
4. THE Sim SHALL verify at generation time that every Plot Stage has at least two independent discovery paths from the Starting Brief (for example a human source and an Intercept), and SHALL regenerate from a derived seed if it does not.
5. WHERE the scenario config enables an internal mole THEN the Sim SHALL designate one Station staff NPC who reports to the Hostile Service.
6. WHEN the player does not supply a seed THEN the Sim SHALL generate one and display it.
7. THE Sim SHALL give every NPC a persona gender, SHALL draw its descriptor only from entries that fit that gender, SHALL make every two Principal NPCs' descriptors differ in at least two elements, and SHALL NOT let two Principal NPCs share a given or family name or two NPCs in one world share a full name.
8. THE Sim SHALL derive every NPC's apparent allegiance from its public post and Cover Story, so that a covert Cell member presents as neutral, a hostile officer presents as the hostile power only in a declared hostile-power post, and a mole presents as Station staff.

### Requirement 2: Ground Truth Separation

**User Story:** As a player, I want the game to hold a fixed hidden truth that I can uncover, so that deduction is meaningful and the answer cannot be talked out of the game.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Sim SHALL store ground truth only in the Truth Store, which SHALL be accessible only to engine modules.
2. THE UI SHALL read only from the Player View and Case File, which SHALL NOT contain truth values, true allegiances, MICE profiles or concealed Propositions.
3. THE Sim SHALL NOT allow any language model output to create, modify or delete ground-truth facts. Model outputs SHALL only produce utterances, Intents and Claims.
4. WHEN a model output conflicts with ground truth THEN the Sim SHALL record it as a Claim and SHALL NOT alter the world.

### Requirement 3: Time and Plot Progression

**User Story:** As a player, I want the hostile Plot to advance whether or not I act, so that my choices carry time pressure.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Sim SHALL model time as days divided into four phases (morning, afternoon, evening, night), and every player action SHALL cost one or more phases.
2. WHEN time advances THEN the Sim SHALL advance the Plot through its stages according to deadlines and prerequisites.
3. WHEN a Plot Stage executes THEN the Sim SHALL emit its observable traces (meetings, communications, movements) so they can be discovered.
4. IF the player disrupts a Plot Stage prerequisite THEN the Sim SHALL delay, reroute or abort that stage according to Hostile Service doctrine.
5. THE Sim SHALL advance NPC schedules deterministically from the seed and the current state.
6. WHEN a Plot or Side Thread trace executes THEN the Sim SHALL emit a Sim event of the kind the trace's template declares (meeting, transmission, drop load, drop collection or movement), bound to the role holders, place, Channel and materiel the trace names.
7. THE Sim SHALL count a Plot Stage as disrupted only when a participant in its own traces is arrested or has fled, a Channel its own traces use is compromised, or a delivery its own traces collect is seized.

### Requirement 4: NPC Dialogue via Knowledge Slices

**User Story:** As a player, I want to talk to NPCs in free text and get believable, in-character answers, so that interrogation and rapport-building feel human.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the player opens a conversation THEN the Sim SHALL build the NPC prompt only from that NPC's persona, Agenda, Cover Story, Knowledge Slice, Told List, relationship summary and recent turns.
2. WHEN the player sends a line THEN the `fast` role SHALL classify it into exactly one Intent from the fixed set: ask, pitch-money, pitch-ideology, pitch-coercion, pitch-ego, reassure, threaten, probe, confront, task, small-talk, end.
3. WHEN an Intent is classified THEN the Sim SHALL update trust and suspicion deterministically before the NPC responds.
4. THE Sim SHALL route a turn to the `voice` role when the scene is high-stakes (interrogation, recruitment pitch, confronting a suspected Double Agent), and to the `fast` role otherwise.
5. THE NPC prompt SHALL instruct the model to speak only as the character and to name only entities on the NPC's known-entity list.

### Requirement 5: Secret Containment

**User Story:** As a player, I want NPCs to be unable to reveal what they do not know, so that the mystery cannot be shortcut by prompt tricks.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Sim SHALL include no Proposition outside an NPC's Knowledge Slice in any prompt for that NPC.
2. WHEN NPC output streams THEN the Leak Guard SHALL release text one sentence at a time, and only after checking that the sentence names no registered entity outside the NPC's known-entity set.
3. IF a sentence fails the Leak Guard THEN the Sim SHALL discard the rest of the turn and regenerate with a stricter instruction, up to a configurable retry limit (default 2).
4. IF retries are exhausted THEN the Sim SHALL substitute a persona-appropriate deflection line.
5. THE Sim SHALL always place player text in the user role and never in system content.
6. THE Sim SHALL log every Leak Guard trip, and every Claim that matches a concealed Proposition the speaker does not know (a chance leak), for evaluation.

### Requirement 6: Lie and Cover Story Consistency

**User Story:** As a player, I want lying NPCs to keep their stories straight unless pressured, so that catching a contradiction means something.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Sim SHALL maintain a Told List for every NPC.
2. WHEN building a prompt THEN the Sim SHALL include the Told List as a compact list instead of full past transcripts.
3. WHILE an NPC's cover is intact THEN the prompt SHALL instruct the NPC to stay consistent with its Cover Story and Told List.
4. WHEN the player confronts an NPC with a Case File Claim that contradicts the NPC's Cover Story or Told List THEN the Sim SHALL run a deterministic pressure check. A successful check SHALL move the NPC into a cracking state that changes its Agenda (partial admission, bargaining or flight).
5. IF Claims extracted from an NPC contradict its Told List without a state change THEN the Sim SHALL record a consistency violation for evaluation.

### Requirement 7: Claim Extraction and the Case File

**User Story:** As a player, I want everything I am told to land in a case file as discrete claims, so that I can cross-reference my sources.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a dialogue turn completes THEN the Sim SHALL enqueue an asynchronous extraction job and SHALL NOT block the next player input on it.
2. THE `bookkeeping` role SHALL return Claims as Propositions that use only the Predicate Vocabulary and Entity Registry ids, validated against a JSON schema.
3. WHEN a Claim is extracted THEN the Sim SHALL record its truth value, the speaker's belief and whether it was a deliberate lie in the Truth Store, and SHALL add the Claim to the Case File without those values.
4. WHEN two Case File Claims share subject and predicate THEN the Case File SHALL mark them as corroborating or conflicting, based only on the Claims themselves.
5. IF extraction output fails schema validation THEN the Sim SHALL retry once, and otherwise SHALL attach the raw transcript excerpt to the Case File as an unparsed note.

### Requirement 8: Source Grading

**User Story:** As a player, I want to grade sources and information like a real analyst, so that judgment is a skill the game rewards.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Case File SHALL let the player assign an Admiralty Grade to each Claim.
2. THE Case File SHALL show each source's history: Claims made, player grades, and corroboration and conflict counts.
3. WHEN the game ends THEN the debrief SHALL score the player's grading accuracy against ground truth.

### Requirement 9: Intercepts and Ciphers

**User Story:** As a player, I want to intercept and break real encoded messages, so that cryptanalysis is gameplay rather than narration.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a Plot Stage executes THEN the Cipher Engine SHALL generate Intercepts whose plaintexts encode that stage's Propositions, or deception Propositions chosen by the Hostile Service.
2. THE Cipher Engine SHALL support at least Caesar, Vigenère, columnar transposition, book cipher (keyed to an in-game public text) and one-time pad.
3. THE Cipher Engine SHALL be implemented in deterministic code with no language model involvement.
4. WHERE the difficulty setting allows THEN the Hostile Service SHALL occasionally commit tradecraft errors (such as pad reuse or predictable headers) that make stronger ciphers breakable.
5. WHEN the player submits a key or plaintext THEN the Sim SHALL verify it and, on success, SHALL add the decrypted Propositions to the Case File with source "intercept".
6. THE intercept workbench SHALL provide frequency counts and shift preview as analyst aids.

### Requirement 10: Recruitment and Asset Handling

**User Story:** As a player, I want to spot, pitch and run Assets, so that building a network is the core loop.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Sim SHALL model each NPC's susceptibility with a hidden MICE profile.
2. WHEN the player makes a pitch Intent THEN the Sim SHALL resolve recruitment deterministically from lever match, trust, suspicion, current Exposure, the offered amount for money pitches, and the seeded PRNG.
3. WHEN recruitment succeeds THEN the NPC SHALL become an Asset that can be tasked to collect on a target, introduce another NPC, service a dead drop, or plant information.
4. WHEN an Asset completes a task THEN the Sim SHALL produce results from ground truth, filtered through the Asset's access and reliability.
5. IF a pitch fails badly THEN the Sim SHALL raise the NPC's suspicion and MAY report the approach to the Hostile Service.
6. THE Sim SHALL give every Asset an access profile (Locations, organisations and persons it can report on) and a reliability value, and WHEN an Asset reports THEN the Sim SHALL return only Propositions concerning entities within its access, with omissions and distortions at a rate set by its reliability.

### Requirement 11: Double Agents and Deception

**User Story:** As a player, I want to uncover, turn and run Double Agents, so that I can play the deception game in both directions.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Sim SHALL support NPCs whose apparent and true allegiances differ.
2. THE Hostile Service SHALL be able to run a Dangle whose reporting mixes verifiable Chickenfeed with deception.
3. WHEN the player turns a hostile agent THEN that agent's true allegiance SHALL change while its apparent allegiance to the Hostile Service remains.
4. WHEN the player feeds Propositions through a doubled agent THEN the Sim SHALL update the Hostile Service's belief model, which MAY change Plot timing, targets or security.
5. THE Hostile Service SHALL be able to quietly double one of the player's Assets after detecting it.

### Requirement 12: Hostile Counterintelligence

**User Story:** As a player, I want the opposition to hunt my network, so that tradecraft choices have consequences.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Sim SHALL track Exposure per Asset, increased by contact frequency, meeting-location risk and tasking risk.
2. WHEN each day ends THEN the Hostile Service SHALL run deterministic detection checks on each Asset.
3. WHEN an Asset is detected THEN the Hostile Service SHALL choose to arrest, double or feed it, according to doctrine and current state.
4. WHERE an internal mole exists THEN the Hostile Service SHALL periodically receive a summary of the player's Case File and adapt the Plot.
5. THE Sim SHALL track Cover Suspicion, increased by detected surveillance, visits to high-risk Locations and detected meetings, and IF it exceeds the configured threshold THEN the player SHALL be burned.

### Requirement 13: Player Actions and Interface

**User Story:** As a player, I want a clear text interface with a case board and workbench, so that I can manage a complex investigation.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE UI SHALL provide these actions: talk, approach, travel, arrange meeting, surveil, follow, service dead drop, intercept, decrypt, read document, send cable, task Asset, pay, grade Claim, link Claims, confront with Claim, turn agent, feed turned agent, request arrest, wait, add note, help.
2. THE UI SHALL show the current day and phase, the current Location, the active scene, and the phase cost and Budget cost of each action before it is taken.
3. THE UI SHALL provide a Case File browser that can be filtered by entity, source and grade.
4. THE UI SHALL stream NPC dialogue and Narrator Flavour sentence by sentence as the Leak Guard releases it.
5. THE engine SHALL expose a UI-agnostic API so that the terminal client can be replaced later.
6. THE UI SHALL render Fact Lines and Flavour in visually distinct styles, so that the player can always tell them apart.
7. WHEN the game ends THEN the UI SHALL show a game-over screen stating the outcome (success, Plot failure or burned) and the end day and phase, and SHALL offer the debrief.
8. WHEN the player opens the debrief THEN the UI SHALL render every debrief section listed in Requirement 19.6.
9. THE UI SHALL provide a save/load screen listing each save's name, seed, Difficulty Preset, day and phase, and IF the selected save's Content Manifest differs from the loaded one THEN SHALL name the differing packs and versions and leave the current game unchanged.
10. IF the model endpoint is unreachable during dialogue THEN the UI SHALL show a recoverable error screen naming the endpoint and offering retry and save-and-quit.

### Requirement 14: Local Model Routing

**User Story:** As the developer, I want each model role configured independently against LM Studio, so that I can swap models without code changes.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE LLM Gateway SHALL connect to an OpenAI-compatible endpoint defined in config (default: LM Studio on localhost).
2. THE config SHALL map each Model Role to a model id, with per-role temperature, max tokens, timeout and reasoning mode.
3. WHEN the game starts THEN the LLM Gateway SHALL list the available models and report any role whose model is missing.
4. THE LLM Gateway SHALL request schema-constrained structured output for intent classification and claim extraction.
5. THE default config SHALL target the Reference Machine with at most two models resident at 4-bit (a dense `voice` model and an MoE `fast` model).
6. THE default config SHALL map the `narrator` role to the same model as the `fast` role.

### Requirement 15: Latency and Prompt Efficiency

**User Story:** As a player, I want NPC replies and descriptions to start quickly, so that play feels live.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Prompt Builder SHALL order prompt content from most static to most dynamic, so that the server's prefix cache can be reused across turns.
2. THE Prompt Builder SHALL keep NPC prompts within a configurable token budget (default 3,000). When over budget, it SHALL trim recent turns first and Told List detail second.
3. THE Sim SHALL target a time to first released sentence of ≤ 2 s on the `fast` and `narrator` roles and ≤ 5 s on the `voice` role on the Reference Machine, and SHALL record actual timings.
4. THE default config SHALL disable or cap reasoning on the `voice`, `fast` and `narrator` roles.
5. WHEN an action resolves THEN the UI SHALL display its Fact Lines before any Narrator call starts, independent of model availability.
6. WHILE a game session is running THEN the LLM Gateway SHALL append each model call's role, model id, time to first released sentence, total duration and tokens per second to a local metrics log.

### Requirement 16: Resilience

**User Story:** As a player, I want the game to keep working when a model misbehaves, so that sessions are not lost.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. IF the model endpoint is unreachable during dialogue THEN the Sim SHALL pause and show a recoverable error without corrupting state.
2. IF a call exceeds its timeout THEN the LLM Gateway SHALL retry once and then fall back to the `fast` role.
3. WHEN a response is detected as a refusal or an out-of-character meta response THEN the Sim SHALL retry once with a reinforced fiction frame, and otherwise SHALL substitute a persona deflection line.
4. THE Sim SHALL never apply partial results of a failed call to state.
5. IF a Narrator call fails, times out or exhausts its retries THEN the Sim SHALL show the Fact Lines without Flavour and continue without pausing.

### Requirement 17: Save, Load and Replay

**User Story:** As the developer, I want deterministic saves and replays, so that bugs and playthroughs can be reproduced exactly.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Sim SHALL save the World State, Truth Store, Case File, PRNG state, event log and transcripts as a versioned JSON snapshot.
2. WHEN a save is loaded THEN the Sim SHALL restore an identical state.
3. THE LLM Gateway SHALL support a record mode that persists every request and response, and a replay mode that serves recorded responses without a model.
4. WHEN a recorded session is replayed from its seed and action log THEN the Sim SHALL reach an identical final state.
5. THE Sim SHALL record an ordered action log of every player action, dialogue line, Case File operation, model response reference and extraction commit.

### Requirement 18: Model Evaluation Harness

**User Story:** As the developer, I want to compare candidate models on fixed scenes, so that model choice is driven by evidence rather than reputation.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE harness SHALL run scenario fixtures (a seed plus scripted player lines or actions) against one or more model configs.
2. THE harness SHALL measure mechanically: Leak Guard trips, Specifics Guard trips, chance leaks, Told List contradictions, refusal rate, time to first sentence, and tokens per second.
3. THE harness SHALL score in-character quality with the `judge` role against a fixed rubric, and SHALL report the judge model's identity alongside the scores.
4. THE harness SHALL output a comparison report as Markdown and CSV.
5. THE harness SHALL include at least these fixtures: mole interrogation, recruitment pitch, Dangle debrief, confrontation with contradicting evidence, and narration of a surveillance result.

### Requirement 19: Arrests, End Conditions and Debrief

**User Story:** As a player, I want a debrief that reveals what was really going on, so that I can see where my judgment held up or failed.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the player requests an arrest THEN the Sim SHALL grant it only if the Case File holds at least the configured number of corroborated Claims implicating the target.
2. IF the player arrests an NPC who is not part of the Cell THEN the Sim SHALL raise Hostile Service alertness and reduce the player's remaining arrest authority.
3. WHEN the Plot completes its final stage THEN the game SHALL end in failure.
4. WHEN the player disrupts the Plot (by arresting the Cell leader, seizing the Plot's materiel, or forcing an abort) THEN the game SHALL end in success.
5. IF the player is burned THEN the game SHALL end in failure.
6. WHEN the game ends THEN the debrief SHALL reveal true allegiances, the actual Plot timeline, which Claims were lies, which leads were Side Threads or Rumours, Directive results, and the player's grading accuracy.

### Requirement 20: Narration

**User Story:** As a player, I want places, scenes and the results of my actions described vividly, so that the city feels real, while knowing that description never invents facts.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN an action resolves THEN the Sim SHALL represent its results as Observations and render each Observation as a Fact Line from a deterministic template.
2. THE Narrator SHALL receive only the Fact Lines, a scene descriptor (Location description, atmosphere tags, weather, crowd level, and visible persons by name or descriptor) and the player's known-entity list.
3. WHEN Narrator output streams THEN the Leak Guard SHALL release Flavour one sentence at a time, only after checking that the sentence names no registered entity outside the player's known-entity set.
4. WHEN a Flavour sentence contains a day name, clock time, date, numeral or proper name that does not appear in the supplied Fact Lines or scene descriptor THEN the Specifics Guard SHALL reject the sentence.
5. IF a Flavour sentence fails the Leak Guard or Specifics Guard THEN the Sim SHALL discard the rest of that Flavour and regenerate once, and otherwise SHALL show the Fact Lines without Flavour.
6. THE Sim SHALL NOT parse Flavour into Claims, Observations or state changes, and SHALL keep Flavour out of the Case File and the Journal's fact log.
7. WHEN the player first sees a Location in a given phase and crowd band THEN the Sim SHALL generate its Location Flavour once, cache it in the save, and reuse it on later visits in the same phase and crowd band.
8. WHERE the player sets narration to off THEN the Sim SHALL show Fact Lines only.

### Requirement 21: Locations and Movement

**User Story:** As a player, I want the city to be made of real places with their own character and rhythms, so that where and when I act matters.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Sim SHALL give every Location a name, aliases, Location Type, District, description, atmosphere tags, opening hours per phase, a crowd level per phase, a risk rating and the set of actions its Location Type allows.
2. THE Sim SHALL connect Districts with Routes, each with a travel cost of 0 or 1 phase, and travel within a District SHALL cost 0 phases.
3. WHEN the player travels THEN the Sim SHALL compute the cheapest route and advance the clock by its cost.
4. WHERE the player chooses a countersurveillance route THEN the Sim SHALL add one phase to the travel cost and reduce the probability that hostile surveillance follows the player.
5. IF the player attempts an action that the current Location does not allow, or the Location is closed in the current phase, THEN the Sim SHALL reject the action and show the allowed actions or opening hours.
6. THE Sim SHALL generate weather for each day deterministically, and crowd levels SHALL depend on Location Type, phase, day of week and weather.
7. THE Sim SHALL list the persons visible at the player's current Location, by name if known and by descriptor otherwise.
8. THE Sim SHALL mark public Locations as known at game start, and SHALL add a private Location to the known set when a Claim, Document, Observation or visit reveals it.
9. THE core pack SHALL give every Location Type at least six descriptions, and the Sim SHALL generate 5–7 Districts and 16–22 Locations.

### Requirement 22: Meeting NPCs

**User Story:** As a player, I want believable ways to get in front of people, so that building a network starts from what I can actually reach.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Sim SHALL let the player talk to an NPC only when the NPC is present at the player's Location.
2. THE Sim SHALL record a Contact Channel for each NPC the player can reach remotely, created by the Starting Brief, an introduction, a successful Cold Approach or a Walk-in.
3. WHEN the player makes a Cold Approach THEN the Sim SHALL resolve a deterministic first-contact check from Cover Identity fit, Location Type, the NPC's archetype wariness and the seeded PRNG.
4. WHEN a first-contact check succeeds THEN the Sim SHALL open a talk scene and create a Contact Channel.
5. IF a first-contact check fails THEN the Sim SHALL play a short brush-off scene and raise the NPC's suspicion.
6. WHEN an Asset or contact completes an introduce task THEN the Sim SHALL create a Contact Channel to the target and give the target a starting trust derived from the introducer's trust with the target.
7. THE Sim SHALL generate Walk-ins, both genuine and Dangles, as events that contact the Station.

### Requirement 23: Surveillance

**User Story:** As a player, I want to watch places and follow people, so that I can see things for myself instead of relying on what I am told.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the player surveils a Location for 1 or 2 phases THEN the Sim SHALL enumerate the Sim events at that Location in the window and produce an Observation for each event that passes an observation check based on crowd level, weather and the participants' tradecraft.
2. WHEN the player follows a visible NPC THEN the Sim SHALL produce Observations of that NPC's movements and contacts until the NPC enters a Location the player cannot enter, the phase ends, or the player is detected.
3. THE Sim SHALL add each surveillance Observation to the Case File as a Claim with source "surveillance", and every such Claim SHALL be true at its observed time.
4. WHEN an observed person is not in the player's known set THEN the Sim SHALL represent them as an Unidentified Subject with a physical descriptor, reusing the same Unidentified Subject for later sightings of that person.
5. WHEN an Unidentified Subject is identified (by a face-to-face introduction, a Dossier photograph or an Asset report) THEN the Sim SHALL add an IS_ALIAS_OF Claim linking the Unidentified Subject to the named entity.
6. WHEN surveillance or a follow runs THEN the Sim SHALL run a detection check from the targets' security consciousness, crowd level, repeat-surveillance count and the Difficulty Preset.
7. WHEN the player is detected THEN the Sim SHALL raise the targets' suspicion and Cover Suspicion, MAY change the targets' schedules, and SHALL show a "you may have been made" Fact Line with the probability set by the Difficulty Preset.

### Requirement 24: Arranged Meetings and Dead Drops

**User Story:** As a player, I want to run my contacts through meetings and dead drops, so that I can trade convenience against exposure.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the player arranges a meeting with an NPC that has a Contact Channel THEN the player SHALL choose a Location and a slot within the next three days, and the Sim SHALL resolve acceptance deterministically from trust, the NPC's schedule, the Location's opening hours and risk, and the NPC's Agenda.
2. WHEN an accepted meeting slot arrives and both parties are at the Location THEN the Sim SHALL open a talk scene.
3. IF the player misses an accepted meeting THEN the Sim SHALL reduce the NPC's trust.
4. WHEN a meeting takes place THEN the Sim SHALL increase the NPC's Exposure from the Location's risk, its crowd level and the NPC's recent contact count.
5. THE Sim SHALL model Dead Drops as concealed sites at Locations whose Location Type allows them, each with an owner and contents.
6. WHEN the player services one of their own Dead Drops THEN the Sim SHALL deliver its contents, accept items left by the player (tasking, payment or Documents), cost one phase, and add less Exposure than a meeting.
7. WHEN the player services a hostile Dead Drop THEN the player SHALL choose to copy the contents and leave them in place, or to seize them, which counts as disrupting any Plot Stage that requires the delivery. Both choices SHALL run a detection check.

### Requirement 25: Interception and Waiting

**User Story:** As a player, I want to collect enemy traffic and to let time pass deliberately, so that signals work and patience are real choices.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Sim SHALL model Channels with a kind (radio, numbers broadcast, courier, dead drop), a schedule and an owner.
2. THE Sim SHALL add a Channel to the player's known Channels when the Starting Brief, a USES_CHANNEL Claim, a Document or an Observation reveals it.
3. WHEN the player intercepts at the Station THEN the Sim SHALL deliver every uncollected transmission on known radio and numbers Channels within the retention window (default two days), each with traffic metadata (time, Channel, length and header).
4. WHEN the player intercepts a courier Channel at a Location on its route during a scheduled window THEN the Sim SHALL produce an Intercept and run a detection check.
5. WHEN the player waits THEN the Sim SHALL advance 1–4 phases at the current Location, deliver events due in that time, and, if the Location is public and open, produce Observations at a reduced observation rate.
6. IF the current Location closes during a wait THEN the Sim SHALL end the wait at closing and show a Fact Line saying so.

### Requirement 26: Starting Brief and Onboarding

**User Story:** As a player, I want a clear opening and help when I need it, so that I can start investigating without reading a manual.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a game starts THEN the Sim SHALL generate a Starting Brief containing a Cover Identity, the Chief of Station, starting known entities, 2–4 initial leads as Claims from HQ Documents, Dossiers on persons of interest, at least one known Channel, at least one player Dead Drop, the starting Directives and the starting Budget.
2. THE Sim SHALL draw every initial lead from the Station's Knowledge Slice, which SHALL include HQ false beliefs at the rate set by the Difficulty Preset.
3. THE discovery-path verifier SHALL use exactly the Starting Brief's known entities, Claims, Channels and Documents as the player's starting knowledge.
4. WHEN a game starts THEN the UI SHALL present the Starting Brief as an in-world Cable and offer an in-person briefing with the Chief of Station.
5. THE UI SHALL provide a help command listing the actions available at the current Location with their costs, and a glossary drawn from the Content Set.
6. WHERE hints are enabled THEN the UI SHALL show a contextual hint the first time each hint trigger occurs, and hints SHALL NOT change Sim state.

### Requirement 27: The Station, Chief and Directives

**User Story:** As a player, I want my own service around me, with a chief who sets objectives and staff who might betray me, so that I am an officer in an organisation rather than a lone detective.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Sim SHALL generate the Station as an organisation with a Chief of Station and 2–3 staff NPCs, each a Principal NPC.
2. THE Sim SHALL issue 1–3 Directives at game start and MAY issue more as the Plot advances, each with an objective the Sim can check, a deadline and a Standing reward.
3. WHEN a Directive's objective is met or its deadline passes THEN the Sim SHALL adjust Standing and send a Cable.
4. WHEN the player sends a trace-request Cable about a known entity or Unidentified Subject THEN the Chief SHALL return a Dossier after the delay set by the Difficulty Preset, with content drawn from the Station's Knowledge Slice.
5. WHEN the player sends a funds-request Cable THEN the Sim SHALL grant an amount determined by Standing.
6. WHERE a mole is enabled THEN the mole SHALL have access to Case File summaries and Cables, and the verifier SHALL confirm two independent discovery paths to the mole's identity.

### Requirement 28: Operational Budget

**User Story:** As a player, I want money to be a limited resource, so that paying sources and running a network involve trade-offs.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Sim SHALL keep the Budget as a ledger of credits and debits in a single currency.
2. THE Sim SHALL debit the Budget for money pitches, payments to Assets, bribes, safehouse rental and task expenses, at the amount shown to the player before confirmation.
3. IF a debit exceeds the balance THEN the Sim SHALL reject the action and leave state unchanged.
4. IF a money-motivated Asset's retainer stays unpaid past the grace period THEN the Sim SHALL reduce that Asset's trust.
5. WHEN the player makes a money pitch THEN the Sim SHALL scale its strength by the offered amount relative to the NPC's money need.

### Requirement 29: Noise, Rumours and Red Herrings

**User Story:** As a player, I want a city full of things that are not the Plot, so that finding the signal takes real deduction.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Sim SHALL generate Background NPCs, each with an archetype, persona, descriptor, schedule and a Knowledge Slice of local facts and Rumours.
2. THE Sim SHALL generate Side Threads from Content Set templates, each with its own true Propositions, traces and comms traffic, and with no Cell member as a participant.
3. THE Sim SHALL generate Rumours as false or distorted Propositions held as false beliefs by Background NPCs and printed in newspapers.
4. THE Cipher Engine SHALL generate Noise Traffic on Channels unrelated to the Plot at the ratio set by the Difficulty Preset.
5. THE Sim SHALL generate all noise from a PRNG stream derived separately from the core world stream, so that changing noise settings leaves the core world unchanged.
6. WHEN noise has been generated THEN the Sim SHALL re-run discovery-path verification, and IF it fails THEN SHALL regenerate the noise from the next derived noise seed.

### Requirement 30: In-World Documents

**User Story:** As a player, I want to read the papers, books, files and cables of the world, so that the city has a written life and clues hide in print.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Sim SHALL generate Documents of these kinds: newspaper, public text, Dossier, Cable and seized material, rendered deterministically from Content Set document templates with no language model involvement.
2. WHEN each day starts THEN the Sim SHALL publish a newspaper edition of 3–6 articles drawn from city events, public Plot traces, Side Thread traces, Rumours and stories planted by the Hostile Service.
3. THE Sim SHALL generate public texts long enough to key every book cipher in use, and SHALL make each key text obtainable at a Location.
4. WHEN the player reads a Document for the first time THEN the Sim SHALL add its asserted Propositions to the Case File as Claims with source "document".
5. THE Sim SHALL compose Dossiers only from the Station's Knowledge Slice and apparent information.

### Requirement 31: Content Packs

**User Story:** As the developer, I want all game content to come from versioned data packs, so that later specs can add archetypes, places, plots and documents without code changes.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Sim SHALL load NPC archetypes, Location Types, Plot templates, Side Thread templates, document templates, persona libraries, descriptor libraries, word lists, Cover Identities, Rumour templates, hint definitions, Difficulty Presets and predicate definitions from Content Packs.
2. WHEN a Content Pack is loaded THEN the loader SHALL validate every file against its schema and every cross-reference, and IF any check fails THEN SHALL refuse to start and report the pack, file and path of each error.
3. THE loader SHALL order packs by declared dependencies with ties broken by pack id, and IF a dependency is missing, has an incompatible version or forms a cycle THEN SHALL refuse to start.
4. IF two packs define the same id without an explicit override THEN the loader SHALL refuse to start.
5. THE loader SHALL produce a Content Manifest listing each pack's id, version and content hash.
6. WHEN a save is loaded whose Content Manifest differs from the loaded one THEN the Sim SHALL refuse to load it and name the differing packs.
7. THE slice SHALL ship a core pack that alone provides all content the slice needs, including at least three Plot templates, four Side Thread templates and twelve Rumour templates.
8. WHEN a new pack uses only existing content kinds THEN the Sim SHALL load and use its content with no code change.

### Requirement 32: Predicate Vocabulary Definitions

**User Story:** As the developer, I want predicates defined as data, so that new kinds of facts can be added in a pack while staying renderable, extractable, encodable and checkable.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Predicate Vocabulary SHALL be defined by predicate definitions in Content Packs, and the core pack SHALL define the initial 15 predicates.
2. EVERY predicate definition SHALL declare its subject and object kinds, its place and time-window rules, an evaluator kind from the built-in set, a second-person template, a third-person template, a unique field-message code and an extractor description.
3. WHEN the Content Set is loaded THEN the Sim SHALL derive the predicate renderers, the Claim Extractor schema, the field-message codes and truth evaluation from the predicate definitions.
4. IF a predicate definition names an unknown evaluator kind, omits a template or reuses a field-message code THEN the loader SHALL refuse to start.

### Requirement 33: Player Aids

**User Story:** As a player, I want a journal, a map and a list of people I know, so that I can keep a complex investigation in my head.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Journal SHALL record the Fact Lines of every action, meeting, Cable and delivered event, grouped by day and phase.
2. THE Journal SHALL let the player add notes attached to a day, an entity or a Claim.
3. THE Map view SHALL show known Locations by District, Routes with travel costs, opening hours, risk ratings, current crowd levels, known Dead Drops and the last visit to each Location.
4. THE People view SHALL show each known person and Unidentified Subject with name or descriptor, apparent affiliation, linked aliases, last sighting, Asset status, a rapport band and Claim counts.
5. THE Sim SHALL build the Journal, Map view and People view only as Player View projections.

### Requirement 34: Difficulty Settings

**User Story:** As a player, I want to choose how hard the game is in concrete terms, so that I can match the challenge to my skill.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE scenario config SHALL select a Difficulty Preset (easy, standard or hard) from the Content Set, or provide custom values.
2. EVERY Difficulty Preset SHALL define: Plot stage count and deadline slack, Background NPC count, Side Thread count, Rumour count, Noise Traffic ratio, HQ false-belief rate, Hostile Service doctrine ranges, detection base rates, the "made" reveal probability, tradecraft-error probability, allowed ciphers, arrest threshold, wrongful-arrest penalty, starting Budget, trace-request delay, Cover Suspicion burn threshold and the hints default.
3. THE UI SHALL show the Difficulty Preset and seed, and the Sim SHALL record both in saves and the Outcome Record.

### Requirement 35: Outcome Record

**User Story:** As the developer, I want each finished game to leave a durable record, so that a future campaign layer can carry consequences forward.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the game ends THEN the Sim SHALL write an Outcome Record to a location separate from saves.
2. THE Outcome Record SHALL contain the outcome, end day, seed, generator version, Content Manifest, Difficulty Preset, Standing, Directive results, surviving Assets (id, archetype, persona, lever, trust, Exposure and doubled flag), Cover Identity status, Cover Suspicion, Hostile Memory and remaining Budget.
3. THE Sim SHALL derive the Outcome Record deterministically from the final state and SHALL validate it against a versioned schema before writing.

### Requirement 36: Turning Hostile Agents

**User Story:** As a player, I want to turn hostile agents I have caught or cornered, so that I can run them back against their own service.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Sim SHALL allow the turn-agent action against an NPC only WHEN the NPC is in Station Custody, or the player has observed the NPC's cover state move to cracking or blown, or the player is in a talk scene with the NPC and the Case File holds at least one corroborated Implicating Claim against the NPC.
2. THE Sim SHALL decide turn-agent eligibility only from Player View and Case File data.
3. WHEN the player attempts to turn an agent THEN the Sim SHALL resolve the attempt deterministically from the chosen MICE lever, any offered amount, the leverage type, trust, suspicion, the NPC's loyalty and the seeded PRNG.
4. IF the target's true allegiance is not the Hostile Service THEN the turn attempt SHALL fail.
5. WHEN a turn attempt fails THEN the Sim SHALL show the same refusal Fact Line regardless of the target's true allegiance and SHALL raise the target's suspicion.
6. WHEN a turn attempt succeeds THEN the Sim SHALL change the NPC's true allegiance to the Station, keep its apparent allegiance as the Hostile Service, make it an Asset marked as turned, and leave the Hostile Service's belief in the agent's loyalty unchanged.
7. WHEN a turned NPC is released from Station Custody THEN the Sim SHALL raise the Hostile Service's suspicion of that NPC in proportion to the phases spent in Custody.

### Requirement 37: Feeding Turned Agents

**User Story:** As a player, I want to choose what a turned agent tells their handlers, so that I can build the agent's credibility and then deceive the Hostile Service.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the player feeds a turned agent THEN the player SHALL choose 1–3 Propositions, each taken from a Case File Claim or composed from the Predicate Vocabulary using only entities, Locations and Channels in the player's known set.
2. IF a fed Proposition breaks its predicate's argument rules or references an entity outside the player's known set THEN the Sim SHALL reject the feed and leave state unchanged.
3. WHEN the turned agent next contacts the Hostile Service THEN the Sim SHALL deliver the fed Propositions and classify each as Chickenfeed if it holds in the Truth Store and as deception otherwise.
4. WHEN fed Propositions are delivered THEN the Hostile Service SHALL raise the agent's credibility for each Proposition it can confirm, lower the agent's credibility and raise its suspicion for each Proposition it can refute, and adopt each unverifiable Proposition as a belief IF the agent's credibility meets the doctrine adoption threshold.
5. WHEN the Hostile Service adopts a belief THEN the Sim SHALL apply the matching belief-driven adaptation rule (reroute, change Channel, change Cell security, or add Abort Pressure).
6. THE Player View SHALL exclude the Chickenfeed or deception classification and every Hostile Service belief update.

### Requirement 38: Plot Abort

**User Story:** As a player, I want a clear way to force the Hostile Service to abandon the Plot, so that disruption is a winnable path rather than luck.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Sim SHALL track Abort Pressure for the Plot.
2. WHEN a Plot Stage is disrupted (a required delivery seized, a required participant arrested or fled, or a required Channel believed compromised by the Hostile Service) THEN the Sim SHALL add 1 to Abort Pressure once per distinct disruption and draw the stage's onDisrupted response.
3. WHEN the Hostile Service adopts a belief that the Station knows or suspects the Plot's target or a Cell member THEN the Sim SHALL add 1 to Abort Pressure once per distinct belief.
4. WHEN Abort Pressure reaches the doctrine abort tolerance, the Cell leader's suspicion reaches the doctrine abort threshold, the Plot's key materiel is seized, or a disruption draws abort or has no reroute available THEN the Sim SHALL abort the Plot.
5. THE Sim SHALL abort the Plot only on a trigger listed in criterion 4.
6. WHEN the Plot aborts THEN the game SHALL end in success.

### Requirement 39: Notifications and Off-screen Events

**User Story:** As a player, I want to learn about things that happen while I am elsewhere in the way an officer really would, so that I am informed without being handed hidden truth.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Sim SHALL mark every Sim event kind as player-visible (addressed to the player or the Station) or hidden.
2. WHEN a player-visible event occurs THEN the Sim SHALL deliver a Notification at the end of the Turn Transaction in which it occurs and SHALL write its Fact Line to the Journal.
3. THE Sim SHALL deliver Notifications for Cable arrivals, Directive issue and resolution, meeting replies (accepted or declined), meeting no-shows by the other party, Walk-in contacts, the daily newspaper, unserviced player Dead Drops, Assets gone silent, and retainers falling due.
4. THE Sim SHALL deliver no Notification for a hidden event, including Plot Stage execution, Hostile Service detection, quiet doubling of an Asset, arrests by the Hostile Service and Hostile Service belief changes.
5. WHEN a hidden event has a player-observable consequence (an arrested Asset missing a meeting or leaving a drop unserviced, an Asset with no contact for the configured silence period, a public arrest or public Plot trace printed in the newspaper) THEN the Sim SHALL deliver the Notification for that consequence only.
6. THE Player View SHALL expose undismissed Notifications as a typed list, which the status bar SHALL show as alerts.
7. THE Sim SHALL build every Notification only from player-visible events and Player View data.

### Requirement 40: Arrest Evidence

**User Story:** As a player, I want to know exactly what counts as evidence against a target, so that building an arrest case is a deliberate analytic task.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Sim SHALL decide whether a Claim implicates a target only from Case File Claims, the Starting Brief and the predicate definitions' implication rules, and never from the Truth Store.
2. THE Sim SHALL treat a Claim as an Implicating Claim against a target WHEN the target, or an Unidentified Subject linked to the target by held IS_ALIAS_OF Claims, fills the role named by the predicate's implication rule and the other argument satisfies that rule's hostile-mark condition.
3. THE core pack SHALL define implication rules for MEMBER_OF, WORKS_FOR, REPORTS_TO, MEETS_AT, CARRIES, SUPPLIES, USES_CHANNEL, PLANS and TARGETS.
4. WHEN the arrest gate counts evidence THEN the Sim SHALL count distinct implicating Propositions whose Claims are marked corroborated.

### Requirement 41: Configuration Files

**User Story:** As the developer, I want scenario and model settings in validated config files, so that tuning needs no code change and mistakes are caught at startup.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the game starts THEN the Sim SHALL read `config/scenario.yaml` and validate it against a schema covering Difficulty Preset selection and overrides, the mole toggle, packs to load, narration mode, hints, recruitment and turning weights, retry limits, the prompt token budget and the metrics log path.
2. IF `config/scenario.yaml` or `config/models.yaml` fails validation THEN the Sim SHALL refuse to start and report each error with its file and field path.
3. THE slice SHALL ship default `config/scenario.yaml` and `config/models.yaml` files that pass validation.

### Requirement 42: Turn Transactions

**User Story:** As the developer, I want each player turn to commit atomically, so that failures never leave the game half-updated and replays stay exact.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Sim SHALL process each player action or dialogue line as one Turn Transaction that commits its Sim state changes, Journal entries, action-log entries and Notifications together.
2. WHEN an action resolves THEN the Sim SHALL commit its Turn Transaction before any Narrator call starts.
3. WHEN an NPC reply finishes streaming, or is replaced by a deflection line, THEN the Sim SHALL commit the dialogue Turn Transaction.
4. IF a model call fails before commit THEN the Sim SHALL discard the whole Turn Transaction, restore the pre-turn state and PRNG state, and on retry SHALL re-run the turn from that state.
5. THE Sim SHALL commit each extraction result as a separate transaction at a turn boundary, in turn order.
6. IF a Narrator call fails after commit THEN the Sim SHALL keep the committed turn unchanged.

### Requirement 43: Model Manager

**User Story:** As the developer, I want the app to manage LM Studio models through its SDK — checking downloads, estimating memory, and loading the active profile explicitly — so that launching the game sets up the right models reliably without a surprise multi-gigabyte download.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Model Manager SHALL connect to LM Studio through the official TypeScript SDK (`@lmstudio/sdk`), and WHEN the local server is not running THEN it SHALL start it before connecting.
2. WHEN the Model Manager runs its startup preflight THEN it SHALL compare the active profile's models against the set LM Studio reports as downloaded, and IF any are missing THEN it SHALL NOT download them silently; instead it SHALL print the exact `lms get` command(s) and fail the preflight, and SHALL perform the download only when the operator runs an explicit `pnpm models:pull`.
3. BEFORE loading a model THE Model Manager SHALL estimate its memory requirement at the configured context length (the SDK's estimate-only path), and IF the active profile's resident models would not fit THEN it SHALL refuse to load and report the shortfall rather than let macOS fall back to a partially-GPU-resident load.
4. THE Model Manager SHALL load each model in the active profile explicitly through the SDK with a stable identifier and the configured context length, SHALL load a model that serves two roles only once, and SHALL set LM Studio so a resident model is not auto-evicted or idle-unloaded while the game runs.
5. AFTER loading THE Model Manager SHALL verify every resident model is fully GPU-resident (via the SDK's loaded-model listing), and IF a model is only partially on the GPU THEN it SHALL warn with the model identifier.
6. THE Model Manager SHALL unload the current profile's models and load another profile's on request, so the evaluation harness can switch profiles (Requirement 18) between runs.
7. THE identifier the Model Manager loads a model under SHALL be the model name the LLM Gateway sends to the OpenAI-compatible endpoint, so that `config/models.yaml` references stable identifiers and inference stays on the portable endpoint rather than the SDK.
8. THE Model Manager preflight SHALL run before the first model call; IF it fails (server unreachable, a model missing, or insufficient memory) THEN the game SHALL refuse to start and report the cause, consistent with the config-validation startup contract (Requirement 41.2).
